## English

# 🏗️ The server has a new layout
We rebuilt the Discord around how the community uses it: fewer channels, clearer paths and room for more languages.

## What's new
- **📌 START** — welcome, rules, how to play, FAQ and about in one place
- **Languages** — chats are split by language: **ENGLISH** and **РУССКИЙ**
- **🆘 HELP** — guides, {#🆘・support} for questions, {#🎫・tickets} for private matters
- **🎥 MEDIA** — screenshots, videos, streams and creators
- **Translations** — info posts, announcements and polls are copied into each language category
- **🔊 VOICE** — join {#➕・Duo} or {#➕・Trio}, the bot opens a room for your party; `/room` locks it, sets a limit or renames it

## What to do
- Don't see a chat? Pick your languages in **Channels & Roles** at the top of the channel list

## 🗳️ Vote
Do you like the new layout? Vote in the poll right below this post. Missing something? Tell us in {#💡・ideas}.
